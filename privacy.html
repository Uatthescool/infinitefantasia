PRIVACY POLICY

Infinite Fantasia · last updated October 23, 2026

The short version

Infinite Fantasia is a free prototype made by one developer, Uatthescool. It keeps only what it needs to save your progress and help balance the game. There are no ads, no tracking cookies, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Playing as a guest

A guest's progress is saved on your own computer (in the browser's storage on the website) and never leaves it, except for run reports (below).

Accounts

If you make an account, the game keeps:

  • your username, which is how you log in;
  • your email address, used only to send a link if you forget your password;
  • your password, stored scrambled (hashed) so nobody, including the developer, can read it;
  • your saved progress: your Avatar, Class Book, Stable, Outfits, run totals and recent run reports, so you can play on any computer;
  • when the account was made and last saved, and the technical records the account service keeps to run safely, such as sign-in times and IP addresses.

Accounts and saves are stored with Supabase, which runs the game's database. Each player can read and change only their own save.

Run reports

To help balance the game, each run you finish (or leave) can be sent to the developer as a report: your Avatar's name, a random player id, and what happened in the run (classes, choices, fights, where it ended, how long it took). Reports go to a Google Sheet through Google Apps Script. You can turn this off at any time in Options, under Share my runs.

The website

The website and the browser version of the game are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security. The site itself sets no cookies.

Your choices

  • Delete your account in the game: Options, then Delete Account. This removes your account, email and cloud save right away. Your computer keeps a copy of your progress as a guest.
  • Erase progress on a computer: Options, then Restart Account, or delete the profile from the Profiles screen.
  • Stop run reports: Options, then Share My Runs: Off.
  • For anything else, such as removing run reports already sent, ask Uatthescool on the game's Discord server.

Children

The game isn't meant for children under 13, and accounts shouldn't be made for them. If a child under 13 has made one, delete it in Options or ask the developer to.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date.
